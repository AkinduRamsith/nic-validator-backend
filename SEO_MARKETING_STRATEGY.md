# 📈 Mint Invoiz SEO & Marketing Strategy

## 🎯 SEO Content Strategy

### Blog Post Ideas

1. **"10 Essential Invoice Best Practices for Small Businesses"**
   - Target keywords: invoice best practices, small business invoicing, professional invoices
   - Focus on actionable tips for creating professional invoices
   - Include templates and examples

2. **"How to Choose the Right Invoicing Software for Your Business"**
   - Target keywords: invoicing software comparison, best invoice software, invoice management tools
   - Compare features, pricing, and use cases
   - Position Mint Invoiz as the ideal solution

3. **"Invoice Automation: Save 10 Hours Per Week on Billing Tasks"**
   - Target keywords: invoice automation, automated billing, time-saving invoicing
   - Highlight automation features and ROI benefits
   - Include case studies and time-saving calculations

4. **"Understanding Invoice Terms: Net 30, Due on Receipt, and More"**
   - Target keywords: invoice payment terms, net 30 meaning, invoice due dates
   - Educational content about payment terms
   - Build authority and trust

5. **"The Complete Guide to Invoice Design and Branding"**
   - Target keywords: invoice design, professional invoice template, invoice branding
   - Visual guide with examples and design tips
   - Showcase Mint Invoiz customization features
